On-Demand Service Marketplace & Partner Companion Suite

Engineered an end-to-end two-sided service marketplace ecosystem consisting of a Customer Booking App and a dedicated Provider/Partner Application for on-demand home maintenance, technicians, and local product ordering.

Architecture Two-Sided Marketplace (Dual Apps)
Role Lead Full-Stack Mobile Architect
Platform Flutter iOS & Android (Unified Codebase)
Backend & Payments Firebase Firestore + Stripe Connect

Executive Summary & Two-Sided Ecosystem

Modern on-demand service platforms require two interconnected mobile applications operating in real time: a Customer Booking App for browsing verified professionals (electricians, plumbers, appliance repair, tutoring, home cleaning) and an accompanying Partner / Provider App (similar to Urban Company, TaskRabbit, or Zowfity/Zowmates) for skilled tradespeople to accept work orders, manage calendars, and receive payouts.

The platform demanded instant sub-second push dispatching, localized geofenced discovery, bidirectional chat between customer and technician, and automated split-fee escrow payments compliant with financial marketplace regulations.

Dual-App Architecture & State Synchronization

To accelerate feature velocity across both client applications, a shared Flutter domain module was established with Riverpod for reactive state injection:

  • Customer Mobile Application: Features category exploration, real-time availability calendar scheduling, technician rating reviews, upfront price estimators, live in-app route tracking, and secure credit/debit card checkout.
  • Partner / Provider Mobile Application: Equips service professionals with audio-visual incoming job dispatch alerts, one-tap accept/decline toggles, customer location navigation via Google Maps, job stage tracking (En Route, In Progress, Completed), and a daily earnings withdrawal dashboard.
  • Real-Time Firebase Firestore & Functions: Document-level security rules govern access between customers and contractors. Serverless Cloud Functions coordinate automated state transitions, invoice generation, and dispute mediation.

Key Technical Accomplishments

  • Stripe Connect Marketplace Payouts: Automated split-fee settlement: collecting payment from the customer, deducting marketplace commission fees, and automatically releasing payouts to the partner's verified bank account upon job completion.
  • In-App Encrypted Chat & Masked Calling: Integrated real-time messaging with image sharing (for sending photos of damaged wiring or piping) and privacy-masked VoIP contact options.
  • Geofenced Dispatch Radius Engine: Implemented spatial queries (Geohashes) to notify only technicians actively on-duty within a 10km radius of the customer's residence.

Production Deliverables & Impact

The dual-application suite delivered scalable commercial performance:

  • Under 15-Second Partner Job Acceptance: Rapid dispatch notifications cut customer waiting times significantly.
  • Dual Store Publication: Successfully published both Customer and Partner apps to Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
  • Zero Payment Friction: Automated escrow releases eliminated manual contractor settlement disputes.
