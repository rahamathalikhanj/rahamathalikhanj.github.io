Ajman Taxi, Sixth Limo & Fytch: UAE Smart Mobility Ecosystem

Enterprise-scale taxi dispatching, scheduled luxury charters, and live GPS telemetry across the United Arab Emirates.

Client Dubai Technologies, UAE
Role Senior Software Engineer (Lead Client Role)
Platform Stack Flutter, Dart (iOS & Android)
State Management Riverpod & BLoC Architecture

Project Overview

Engaged by Dubai Technologies in the UAE, this initiative encompassed the design, development, and scaling of three flagship mobility applications: Ajman Taxi (government-regulated public taxi hailing), Sixth Limo (luxury premium chauffeur booking), and Fytch (corporate fleet mobility).

Serving a high volume of active riders across Dubai, Sharjah, and Ajman, the system required a shared cross-platform core capable of running with native performance on both Android and iOS devices, handling continuous bi-directional socket telemetry and multi-currency billing.

Business Problem & Challenges

  • High-Frequency Driver Telemetry: Managing thousands of simultaneous vehicle GPS updates without UI rendering jank or excessive battery consumption on user devices.
  • Complex Booking Modes: Seamlessly supporting instant street dispatch, scheduled future rides, multi-destination corporate itineraries, and automated recurring bookings.
  • Multi-Channel Payment Compliance: Integrating UAE-specific and global payment gateways including Stripe, PayPal, and Google Pay with automated VAT-compliant invoicing.
  • Reliable Cross-Platform Release Cadence: Delivering bi-weekly production releases across both Google Play and Apple App Store with zero regressions.

Technical Architecture & Engineering Solution

To satisfy both extreme responsiveness and unified code maintainability, the application utilized a feature-first modular Flutter architecture:

  • State Management Combination: Leveraged BLoC for transactional, predictable ride state machines (Initial → Searching → Dispatched → In-Trip → Completed) and Riverpod for scoped dependency management and real-time streaming state.
  • Map Engine & Marker Smoothing: Built custom smooth marker animation pipelines on top of Google Maps SDK and HERE Maps API, interpolating lat/lng coordinates and bearing angles to ensure vehicle icons glide smoothly along roadways.
  • Real-Time Communications: High-throughput WebSockets connection layer with automated exponential backoff reconnection, decoupled behind a reactive stream repository.
  • CI/CD Automation: Configured automated GitLab CI/CD pipelines for static analysis, unit testing, automated Android keystore signing, and staging deployment.

  • Utilized AI-assisted engineering tools for automated regression analysis, API mock testing, and refactoring legacy modules.
