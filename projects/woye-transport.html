Public Booking (Woye Transport)

Engineered an on-demand ride-hailing and driver tracking mobile application tailored for Ethiopia's telecommunications infrastructure, featuring low-data consumption protocols and robust live WebSocket vehicle tracking.

Client / Market Woye Transport, Ethiopia
Role Senior Android Engineer
Platform Native Android (Java & Kotlin)
UI & DI Jetpack Compose + Hilt DI

Executive Summary & Emerging Market Challenge

In rapidly urbanizing cities like Addis Ababa, on-demand ride hailing must perform across a diverse device landscape consisting primarily of budget and mid-tier Android smartphones operating on bandwidth-constrained 3G/4G cellular networks. Users face high mobile data tariffs and periodic network jitter.

Woye Transport required a high-performance passenger mobile app that could provide real-time fare transparency, immediate nearest-driver dispatch, and live route tracking while minimizing battery consumption and keeping network telemetry payload sizes strictly under control.

Modular Android Architecture & Hybrid Migration

The application codebase underwent an architectural modernization from legacy imperative Android views to modular MVVM powered by Jetpack Compose and Hilt:

  • Gradual Jetpack Compose Migration: Modernized interactive booking sheets and driver confirmation cards to declarative Jetpack Compose composables, reducing layout boilerplate by 45% while preserving tested background location services.
  • Payload-Optimized WebSocket Protocol: Designed a lightweight binary-friendly coordinate serialization format for driver telemetry frames, reducing raw network data transfer by over 60% compared to standard verbose JSON payloads.
  • Hilt Dependency Injection: Implemented compile-time dependency injection across all ViewModels, Use Cases, and Repository singletons, establishing clean architectural testability and rapid feature development.

Key Technical Accomplishments

  • Aggressive Low-RAM Optimization: Profiled memory allocations to prevent Out-Of-Memory (OOM) errors on devices with 2GB RAM. Implemented aggressive bitmap recycling for vehicle category graphics and cached map styles.
  • Localized Tariff & Currency Engine: Architected dynamic fare calculation supporting local payment options, cash payment reconciliation, and automated tariff adjustments during peak rain or traffic periods.
  • Resilient Re-connection Handshake: Developed an exponential reconnect strategy that automatically re-establishes ride tracking sessions without requiring passenger app reloads when cellular connectivity drops and resumes.

Production Deliverables & Impact

Woye Transport established a reliable mobile mobility platform in the region:

  • 60% Less Mobile Data Consumed: Streamlined payload protocols saved passengers substantial mobile data costs during daily commutes.
  • 99.5% Crash-Free Session Rate: Maintained across a wide spectrum of entry-level Android hardware.
  • Rapid Driver Connection: Average ride matching confirmation delivered in under 8 seconds.
