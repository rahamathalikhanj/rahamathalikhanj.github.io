Fleet Transport Service (FTS Pro & EHS)

Engineered an enterprise fleet management and logistics platform featuring multi-level managerial vehicle approval workflows, fuel telemetry auditing, and a 100% offline-first driver application powered by Isar local DB.

Client Type Enterprise Logistics Client
Role Lead Mobile Architect
Platform Flutter Enterprise (iOS & Android)
Offline Engine Isar Embedded DB + BLoC

Executive Summary & Operational Context

Corporate logistics and heavy vehicle fleet operations face significant operational hazards when drivers operate in industrial zones, remote depots, and desert transit corridors where cellular signal is nonexistent. Traditional cloud-dependent mobile applications frequently lose driver trip logs, odometer sign-offs, and environmental health & safety (EHS) checklist records during signal dead zones.

FTS Pro was commissioned as an enterprise-grade solution to digitize fleet allocation, vehicle inspection sign-offs, pre-trip safety audits, and automated route telemetry. The central technical mandate was an offline-first architecture: drivers must be able to log trip events, complete vehicle checklists, and capture digital signatures without network connectivity, with seamless automated background reconciliation once connection is restored.

Offline-First Architecture & Data Sync

The system implements a local-first reactive model using the high-performance Isar NoSQL Database embedded directly within the Flutter engine:

  • Local-First Isar Database Storage: All user actions—start trip, log fuel receipt, odometer checkpoint, damage photo, driver signature—are written immediately to Isar DB with microsecond latency. The UI renders directly from local streams, providing instant response regardless of network quality.
  • Bi-Directional Sync Queue Engine: A custom sync worker evaluates network reachability, batches offline transactions into compressed payloads, and coordinates with backend REST APIs using idempotency keys to eliminate duplicate transaction submissions.
  • Conflict Resolution Matrix: Implemented deterministic conflict resolution policies (last-write-wins with managerial priority override) ensuring dispatch changes made on the central operations web portal do not overwrite on-duty driver logs.

Key Technical Accomplishments

  • Multi-Tier Vehicle Approval Hierarchy: Engineered dynamic approval workflows where vehicle requests route conditionally through department supervisors, fleet managers, and security gatekeepers based on vehicle class and trip duration.
  • Fuel & Odometer Telemetry Auditing: Integrated camera image capture with local OCR pre-processing to validate odometer readings against fuel receipt inputs, preventing fraudulent mileage entries.
  • Offline Breadcrumb GPS Logging: Engineered a lightweight background coordinate buffer that records driver route breadcrumbs locally during disconnection and transmits a smoothed route vector upon signal recovery.

Production Deliverables & Impact

FTS Pro transitioned the enterprise client from paper logbooks to a digitized operational standard:

  • 100% Data Retention in Dead Zones: Zero lost trip logs or safety audits across hundreds of daily long-haul routes.
  • 75% Faster Vehicle Dispatch Approvals: Automated notifications and mobile sign-offs replaced manual paper requisition forms.
  • Complete EHS Audit Compliance: Full digital traceability for all pre-trip safety checklists and incident reports.
