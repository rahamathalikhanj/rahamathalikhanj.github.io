DoctorHelp: Telemedicine & Clinic Patient Management System

Streamlining clinical workflows, doctor appointment triage, and electronic health record management with a modern Flutter application.

Project Overview

DoctorHelp was built to replace outdated, cumbersome clinical software with a rapid, elegant, patient-first mobile and tablet application. The platform streamlines doctor consultation scheduling, patient medical history, digital prescriptions, and telemedicine triage.

Engineering Challenges & Bottlenecks

  • Strict Privacy & Data Security: Safeguarding sensitive patient health information (PHI) with end-to-end encryption complying with HIPAA and local data protection regulations.
  • Intuitive UI for Diverse Patient Demographics: Designing an accessible interface easily navigable by elderly patients and busy doctors alike.
  • Instant Clinical Prescriptions: Eliminating illegible handwritten notes through standardized digital e-prescriptions.

Architecture & Solutions

  • Edge-to-Edge Material 3 UI: Engineered transparent status and system navigation bar styling in Flutter with Plus Jakarta Sans typography, maximizing clinical readability.
  • Encrypted Clinical Repositories: Built an encrypted local caching layer for off-line chart reviews with AES-256 secure storage for biometric patient logins.
  • Automated Queue Management: Implemented real-time waiting room queue tracking, reducing patient waiting anxiety through live SMS/push status updates.
  • WebRTC Consultation Bridge: Integrated an encrypted low-latency video calling module for remote patient follow-ups.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Architected the Flutter clean architecture layers separating presentation, domain, and data.
  • Engineered the patient onboarding and appointment scheduling state machine.
  • Integrated secure PDF generation for digital prescriptions with QR code pharmacy validation.
