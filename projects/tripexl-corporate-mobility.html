TripeXL.AI: Corporate Fleet Mobility & Driver Telematics

Automating corporate employee ground travel, multi-tier managerial approval workflows, and mandatory pre-trip vehicle audits.

Client / Ecosystem Corporate Fleets & Enterprise Travel Desks
Engineering Role Lead Client Software Engineer
Platform Stack Flutter (iOS, Android) & Corporate Web Console
Architecture BLoC & Real-Time Socket Repositories

Project Overview

TripeXL.AI tackled the complexities of corporate ground transportation. Companies frequently suffer from unauthorized employee travel, exaggerated driver mileage claims, and lack of vehicle tracking. TripeXL.AI introduced an end-to-end governed travel workflow connecting employees, managers, dispatchers, and drivers.

Engineering Challenges & Bottlenecks

  • Multi-Tier Corporate Approvals: Handling varied organizational travel policies requiring manager, travel desk, and finance sign-offs before trip allocation.
  • Mileage & Fuel Fraud: Drivers manipulating manual logbooks to claim extra fuel and overtime allowances.
  • Real-Time Fleet Oversight: Giving corporate travel admins visibility over dozens of simultaneous executive airport transfers and site visits.

Architecture & Solutions

  • Mandatory Pre-Trip Odometer Verification: Enforced a strict digital check-in where drivers must photograph the vehicle odometer and fuel gauge with geo-tagging before a trip can start.
  • Policy-Driven Approval Hierarchy: Built a flexible rules engine routing travel requests based on cost center, trip distance, and employee seniority.
  • Smooth Telematics Map Tracking: Developed a battery-efficient vehicle location tracker using Google Maps SDK with bearing angle smoothing.
  • Electronic Trip Billing Reconciliation: Automated trip distance calculation matching GPS actuals against route projections for error-free corporate invoicing.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Architected both the Employee Requestor App and Driver Partner App in Flutter.
  • Engineered the camera capture and image verification pipeline for odometer audits.
  • Integrated real-time WebSocket vehicle telemetry and trip status state machines.
