QuickCart: Multi-Store Quick-Commerce & Retail Platform

Building a high-performance hyperlocal grocery and multi-vendor shopping app delivering sub-second catalog browsing and 15-minute dispatch.

Client / Ecosystem Regional Supermarkets & Quick-Commerce Startups
Engineering Role Lead Android & Mobile Solutions Architect
Platform Stack Native Android (Kotlin & Compose) and Flutter
Architecture Clean Architecture, ViewModel & StateFlow

Project Overview

The QuickCart platform was engineered to bring local brick-and-mortar supermarket networks and dark store operators into the quick-commerce era. The application delivers seamless catalog navigation, instant quantity updates, and precise geofenced inventory routing to ensure items shown are physically present in the nearest fulfillment center.

Engineering Challenges & Bottlenecks

  • Stock Outages During Checkout: Preventing customers from ordering items that were just purchased by in-store shoppers.
  • Complex Multi-Store Catalog Navigation: Displaying distinct inventories and pricing based on the customer's exact delivery pin code.
  • Frictionless Mobile Checkout: Eliminating checkout abandonment through streamlined single-screen payment.

Architecture & Solutions

  • Redis In-Memory Stock Decrement: Implemented atomic Redis inventory counters that reserve stock items the moment the customer enters the checkout screen.
  • Geofenced Dark Store Sharding: Filtered product catalogs dynamically based on delivery radius polygons, preventing out-of-range dispatch.
  • Jetpack Compose Declarative UI: Engineered the Android client using modern declarative Compose UI components, achieving smooth 60 FPS scrolling through thousands of SKU items.
  • Automated Split Payment Escrow: Integrated Stripe and Razorpay Route to split customer payments between the store merchant and the delivery fleet automatically.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Architected the native Android client using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Clean Architecture.
  • Engineered the dark store geofencing and stock allocation microservice.
  • Implemented single-screen checkout with instant UPI intent and tokenized card flows.
