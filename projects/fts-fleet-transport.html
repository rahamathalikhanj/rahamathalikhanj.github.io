Fleet Transport Service (FTS Pro): Enterprise Logistics & Telemetry System

Enterprise fleet management solution featuring multi-level vehicle approval matrices, maintenance tracking, and an offline-first driver app.

Domain Enterprise Logistics & Fleet Mobility
Role Senior Mobile Software Engineer
Platform Stack Flutter & Dart
Offline Storage Isar Embedded NoSQL Database

Project Overview

FTS Pro (Fleet Transport Service & Environmental Health Safety) is a mission-critical mobile solution designed for heavy industrial fleet operators, corporate logistics pools, and field service coordinators.

Operating across extensive geographic regions where drivers frequently navigate through desert corridors, deep cargo ports, and rural industrial estates with zero cellular reception, the entire mobile system was mandated to be fully functional offline without stopping driver operations.

Business Problem & Challenges

  • Zero-Connectivity Dead Zones: Drivers operating in isolated depots or sub-grade loading zones experienced complete cellular dropouts, resulting in lost trip start/stop records and unrecorded cargo handovers.
  • Multi-Level Operational Approvals: Vehicle dispatch required strict compliance sign-offs between facility supervisors, safety inspectors, and drivers prior to vehicle release.
  • Continuous Telemetry & Fuel Auditing: Fleets needed automated odometer readings, fuel consumption tracking, and maintenance warning triggers to curb unauthorized vehicle usage.
  • Database Synchronization Conflicts: Merging offline trip edits and field inspection checklists with central enterprise servers upon network restoration without data collisions.

Technical Architecture & Engineering Solution

To solve the offline bottleneck while maintaining enterprise-grade data integrity, we architected an Offline-First Reactive Framework in Flutter:

  • Embedded High-Speed Local DB: Integrated Isar Database (extremely fast NoSQL engine with compile-time type safety) on the device, ensuring instantaneous UI reads and writes regardless of network state.
  • Sync Engine with Queue Serialization: Developed a background synchronization engine that stores mutations in an encrypted FIFO sync queue. When connectivity transitions to active, the queue streams batch updates to the backend with timestamp-based conflict resolution.
  • BLoC State Isolation: Employed the BLoC (Business Logic Component) pattern to decouple UI presentation from synchronization logic, preventing UI freezes during high-volume offline record syncs.
  • Telemetry & Audit Engine: Background GPS checkpointing and route analytics with local compaction to minimize storage footprint on entry-level Android devices.

Key Contributions by Rahamath Alikhan

  • Integrated RESTful enterprise APIs with customized compression headers, reducing field mobile data usage by 35%.
