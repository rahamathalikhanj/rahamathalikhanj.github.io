Roadaider: On-Demand Emergency Roadside Assistance System

Emergency assistance dispatching platform connecting stranded motorists with nearby tow trucks, battery jump-starts, and fuel delivery.

Product Roadaider On-Demand Platform
Role Android Software Engineer
Platform Stack Native Android (Java, Android SDK)
Core Integration Google Maps GPS & Stripe Billing

Project Overview

Roadaider was conceived to eliminate the anxiety and unpredictability of vehicular breakdowns. When drivers experience unexpected tire blowouts, empty fuel tanks, dead batteries, or mechanical immobilizations on highways, Roadaider delivers instant emergency dispatching at upfront, transparent fixed rates.

The platform pairs stranded users with the nearest equipped emergency service provider, streaming the recovery vehicle's real-time approach directly on an interactive map.

Business Problem & Challenges

  • Critical Stress User Experience: Stranded users are typically in hazardous or high-stress environments, necessitating a clean 3-tap dispatch flow with zero non-essential steps.
  • Battery Conservation During Crisis: Mobile devices of stranded drivers often have limited battery; GPS location polling had to be strictly optimized to prevent device shutdown before rescue arrival.
  • Eliminating Price Haggling: Traditional towing often suffers from unpredictable roadside price surges. The app required transparent upfront fare calculation based on distance and service tier.
  • Unreliable Highway Connectivity: Background service recovery was crucial to ensure requests and location pings survived intermittent cellular packet loss.

Technical Architecture & Engineering Solution

Engineered with native Android Java emphasizing rock-solid background stability and low-resource overhead:

  • Location Architecture: Integrated Google Play Services FusedLocationProviderClient configured with balanced power-accuracy profiles, switching to high-accuracy GPS only during active vehicle approach.
  • Background Resilience: Built background workers and foreground service notifications with custom notification channels, keeping users informed of driver ETA even when the device screen is off.
  • Payment Pipeline: Integrated Stripe Mobile SDK for secure pre-authorized card holds that capture payment only upon verified job completion by the service technician.
  • Real-Time Messaging: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) data messages driving real-time dispatch state changes: En Route, Arrived at Scene, and Service Completed.
