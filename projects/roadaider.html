Road Assistant (Roadaider) Platform

Engineered an emergency roadside dispatch mobile application connecting stranded motorists with nearby tow trucks, battery jump-start units, and fuel delivery services with real-time GPS progress.

Product Type On-Demand Emergency Platform
Role Native Android Engineer
Platform Native Android (Java & SDK)
Key Systems Foreground Location + Stripe + FCM

Executive Summary & Problem Statement

When a vehicle breaks down on a highway or secluded road, distress levels are acute. Traditional roadside clubs rely on call centers with slow response times and no visual confirmation of tow truck dispatch. Roadaider was designed as an "Uber for roadside assistance", enabling drivers to tap their phone screen, select their vehicle breakdown issue (flat tire, dead battery, engine overheating, fuel runout, or collision towing), receive a transparent upfront quote, and watch their assigned technician approach in real time.

The technical challenge centered on maintaining background location services and high-priority push events without battery drain or Android OS task-killer terminations while drivers waited with limited battery charge.

Resilient Android Architecture

To ensure the app would operate reliably under stressful emergency conditions, the mobile client was architected using decoupled Android Service components:

  • Foreground Service Location Tracker: Implemented a sticky Android Foreground Service with an ongoing notification channel. This prevented the Android operating system from killing location polling when users locked their screens or switched to emergency phone calls.
  • High-Precision Geolocation Fallback: Employed Google Play Services Fused Location Provider with multi-source fallback (GPS, Wi-Fi triangulation, and cell tower IDs) to lock customer coordinates even under heavy tree canopies or overpasses.
  • Defensive Crash Isolation: Encapsulated network and mapping dependencies inside decoupled manager classes, ensuring that transient map rendering errors never crash the core emergency call and SMS fallback capabilities.

Key Technical Accomplishments

  • Vehicle Type & Tonnage Matching: Engineered input validation and vehicle model selectors to ensure heavy SUVs and electric vehicles (EVs) are automatically assigned flatbed tow trucks rather than standard wheel-lift hooks.
  • Upfront Transparent Fare Engine: Integrated automated distance-based pricing formulas with Stripe pre-authorization holds, preventing price gouging and ensuring drivers and technicians agree on pricing before dispatch.
  • High-Priority FCM Notifications: Configured Firebase Cloud Messaging with wake-lock triggers, waking the client device to display critical dispatch status transitions ("Technician Dispatched", "1 km Away", "Arrived").

Production Impact & Deliverables

The platform enabled rapid emergency response for thousands of stranded drivers:

  • Average Dispatch Time Under 4 Minutes: Automated GPS matching drastically reduced wait times compared to traditional phone call centers.
  • Zero Lost Dispatches: Guaranteed delivery of status alerts through sticky foreground service notifications.
  • High User Trust: Transparent upfront pricing and live technician tracking reduced customer cancellation rates by 65%.
