Enterprise ERP & Custom CRM Operations Suite

Designed and engineered an integrated Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform uniting responsive web admin dashboards with synchronized field mobile apps.

System Type Custom ERP & CRM Platform
Role Lead Full-Stack Systems Architect
Web Portal React.js + Next.js + TypeScript
Mobile & Database Flutter + PostgreSQL + Azure

Executive Summary & Enterprise Challenge

Growing enterprises and mid-market organizations frequently suffer from siloed operational tools: sales teams track customer leads on disparate spreadsheets, operations managers handle resource approvals via email threads, and field personnel log maintenance on paper forms. This fragmentation causes missed invoices, duplicate equipment scheduling, and zero real-time visibility for executive leadership.

The mandate was to build a unified, high-performance Enterprise ERP and CRM Suite that brings together client onboarding, contract lifecycles, inventory and asset tracking, multi-departmental approval chains, and automated billing into a cohesive web and mobile environment.

Multi-Tenant Web & Mobile Architecture

The platform was engineered using a cloud-native microservices architecture designed for high data integrity, strict auditability, and sub-second analytical reporting:

  • Web Admin & Operations Dashboard (Next.js & React): Server-side rendered (SSR) executive portal with dynamic data grids, Chart.js financial analytics, customer CRM pipelines, inventory management, and bulk CSV/PDF reporting.
  • Field Mobile Application (Flutter): Synchronized cross-platform mobile companion enabling on-site staff and managers to sign off on resource requisitions, scan asset QR/barcodes, and view customer booking tickets on the fly.
  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Multi-tenant security matrix governing permissions across Super Admins, Department Heads, Sales Representatives, and Field Technicians with immutable audit logging.

Key Technical Accomplishments

  • Automated Invoicing & Milestone Billing: Connected CRM deal stages directly to ERP ledger accounts, automatically generating GST/VAT-compliant PDF invoices upon milestone approval.
  • Real-Time WebSocket Notification Bus: Dispatches instant desktop browser alerts and mobile push notifications whenever a requisition requires managerial sign-off or an invoice status changes.
  • PostgreSQL with Timescale Telemetry: Optimized relational schemas with automated database partitioning, indexing, and connection pooling to handle millions of historical asset sensor readings and financial records.

Production Deliverables & Impact

The unified suite revolutionized operational efficiency across the organization:

  • 65% Reduction in Administrative Overhead: Automated approval workflows replaced slow paper trails and back-and-forth email loops.
  • 100% Real-Time Asset Visibility: Executive leadership gained live tracking across thousands of distributed corporate assets and client contracts.
  • Sub-Second Web Dashboard Query Speeds: Highly indexed PostgreSQL databases delivered complex financial reports in under 500ms.
