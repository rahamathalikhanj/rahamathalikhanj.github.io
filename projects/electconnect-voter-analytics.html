ElectConnect: Election Digital Platform & Voter GIS Analytics

Transforming static voter rolls into an indexed geospatial intelligence suite for volunteer canvassing and election day poll tracking.

Client / Ecosystem Election Campaign Committees & Civic Observers
Engineering Role Lead Architect & Mobile Engineer
Platform Stack Flutter (iOS & Android) & Go / PostGIS Backend
Architecture Riverpod, SQLite Offline & PostGIS

Project Overview

Election campaigns have traditionally relied on cumbersome paper voter lists and manual doorstep canvassing, leading to missed households and chaotic election day voter slip distribution. ElectConnect digitized constituency operations with an instant voter roll search engine, booth GIS maps, and live polling telemetry.

Engineering Challenges & Bottlenecks

  • Phonetic Variations in Regional Names: Voters often spell their names differently on electoral rolls compared to identity cards.
  • Strict Security & Anti-Leak Safeguards: Preventing mass scraping of voter databases by unauthorized workers or rival campaigns.
  • Patchy Connectivity at Rural Polling Booths: Canvassers must look up voter serial numbers even when cellular signal drops.

Architecture & Solutions

  • PostgreSQL Trigram Phonetic Indexing: Implemented GIN-indexed trigram similarity search enabling instant phonetic matches across regional spelling variants.
  • PostGIS Polling Booth Bounding Zones: Mapped electoral boundaries and booth locations with PostGIS spatial polygon queries.
  • Offline SQLite Booth Caching: Volunteers download their specific booth's voter register securely, enabling offline serial lookups with zero network lag.
  • Turnout Telemetry Engine: Constructed a real-time polling station reporting system streaming hourly voting percentages to the central war room.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Architected the Flutter volunteer mobile application with offline SQLite synchronization.
  • Constructed the high-throughput voter roll search backend with trigram similarity algorithms.
  • Implemented role-based permissions preventing unauthorized data export.
