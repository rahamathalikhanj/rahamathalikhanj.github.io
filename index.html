Flutter is recommended when you want to target both iOS and Android simultaneously with a single, maintainable codebase, rapid time-to-market, and smooth 60fps animations. Native Android (Kotlin & Jetpack Compose) is ideal if your application requires deep hardware-level integration (custom Bluetooth/BLE sensors, foreground location services, or complex GIS spatial algorithms) or when building specifically for Android-exclusive enterprise handhelds.
