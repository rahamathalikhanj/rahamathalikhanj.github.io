Product Overview & Commercial Value

TurfMate is an end-to-end sports arena management and slot reservation ecosystem designed for turf owners, sports clubs, and athletes to eliminate double-booking headaches and manual phone call reservations.

Players can browse nearby turfs, view real-time hourly slot matrices, split court fees with teammates, and pay via instant digital checkout. Venue owners manage multiple pitches, floodlight scheduling, walk-in bookings, and revenue analytics from a dedicated mobile dashboard.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Dynamic Hourly Slot Grid

Real-time calendar matrix with peak/off-peak pricing, automated buffer times between matches, and instant slot hold timers preventing checkout races.

2. Teammate Split Payments

Allows the team captain to divide the total booking cost among friends via payment links or in-app wallet balance.

3. IoT Smart Court Automation

Optional hardware relay integration that automatically toggles arena floodlights and scoreboards only during active confirmed bookings.

4. Venue Owner Command Center

Mobile and tablet dashboard showing live court occupancy, cash collection registers, monthly revenue graphs, and customer retention metrics.

Technical Specifications
