Executive Limousine & Accessible Chauffeur Booking Software

Premium private hire reservation software tailored for corporate VIP transit, airport limousine meet-and-greet, and certified wheelchair-accessible medical transportation.

Primary Stack Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Hilt, HERE Maps
Target Verticals Chauffeur Fleets, Airport Transfers, Medical Transport
Live Deployment Vimo Services (Singapore)
Payment Security PCI DSS Tokenized Card Storage

Product Overview & Business Utility

Unlike mass ride-hailing apps, luxury chauffeur operations and specialized healthcare transit require strict punctuality guarantees, vehicle capability verification (hydraulic ramp vs. lift for wheelchair access), corporate multi-booking invoicing, and high-precision toll calculation for airport surcharges.

This solution provides a complete luxury reservation experience with flight tracking integration, scheduled hourly itineraries, recurring business bookings, and automated corporate monthly billing.

Key Feature Capabilities

Airport Transfer & Meet-and-Greet

Flight number linking with automatic delay adjustment, complimentary wait time countdown, and customized chauffeur greeting sign display.

Accessible Mobility Verification

Dedicated booking flow for passengers requiring wheelchair ramps, medical escort certifications, and specialized vehicle dimensions.

HERE Maps Dynamic Geofencing

Automatic detection of city electronic toll gantries (ERP), airport zone surcharges, and custom corporate district fixed-rate polygons.

Corporate Invoicing & Stripe

Corporate account billing, automated PDF tax invoicing, PCI DSS compliant card saving, driver tips, and promo voucher management.

Technical Specifications

Mobile Framework Native Android (100% Kotlin & Jetpack Compose UI) with Flutter cross-platform option
Routing & Tolls HERE Maps SDK Mobile & REST Routing with dynamic polygonal geofencing
Architecture 3-Tier Clean Architecture (Presentation, Domain, Data) with Hilt Dependency Injection
Payment Infrastructure Stripe Mobile SDK (3D Secure 2.0), Google Pay, Apple Pay, and Corporate Credit Accounts
Push Notifications Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) high-priority data payloads with fallback SMS channels
