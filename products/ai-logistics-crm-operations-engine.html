Product Overview & Commercial Value

LogiFlow AI is a central operational automation engine engineered for freight forwarders, container logistics brokers, and 3PL providers drowning in manual quotation emails and fragmented WhatsApp updates.

The engine ingests incoming inquiries across Microsoft Outlook and WhatsApp, retrieves live pricing rules from Zybo ERP and rate sheets using pgvector semantic RAG, generates accurate freight quotes in seconds, and orchestrates staff execution via interactive WhatsApp action buttons.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Multi-Channel Inquiry Ingestion

Parses complex multi-container and air cargo RFQ inquiries from incoming Outlook emails and customer WhatsApp messages with NLP entity extraction.

2. Semantic RAG Quotation Engine

Leverages pgvector embeddings over carrier contracts, historical tariffs, and port handling surcharges to compute accurate, margin-protected quotations.

3. WhatsApp Conversational Operations

Staff receive urgent job alerts and approval prompts directly inside WhatsApp, updating shipment status with simple button taps.

4. ERP & CRM Bi-Directional Sync

Bi-directional live synchronization between Zybo ERP, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Google Sheets master schedules.
