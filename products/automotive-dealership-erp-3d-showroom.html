Product Overview & Commercial Value

MotoSaaS is a next-generation dealership management software engineered specifically for two-wheeler OEM dealers, multi-brand auto retailers, and electric vehicle (EV) startups to replace fragmented legacy billing software with an integrated omni-channel ecosystem.

Featuring an interactive 3D WebGL vehicle customizer, prospective buyers can inspect 360-degree exterior finishes, swap accessory configurations, and schedule test rides directly from their phones. Simultaneously, dealership staff manage multi-branch vehicle stock, lead pipelines, insurance renewals, and service workshop job cards from a unified console.

Key Architectural Modules

1. 3D Interactive Vehicle Showroom

Photorealistic glTF/GLB 3D model visualizer supporting real-time paint shader swapping, accessory fitting, dynamic lighting, and graceful 2D image fallbacks on low-end devices.

2. Dual-Mode Customer & Staff Mobile App

Single Flutter binary delivering an engaging shopping experience for guests while seamlessly transitioning to staff operational workflows upon authenticated corporate login.

3. Service Workshop & Job Card Management

Digital repair order generation, technician bay allocation, vehicle inspection checklist, spare parts billing, and automated customer service progress SMS/WhatsApp alerts.

4. Multi-Branch Inventory & Spare Parts

Real-time tracking of chassis numbers, engine numbers, battery serials (for EVs), parts re-order thresholds, and inter-branch stock transfer authorizations.

Technical Specifications

Backend Architecture ASP.NET Core 8 modular monolith utilizing Clean Architecture and CQRS principles
Mobile Framework Flutter cross-platform supporting custom platform channels and offline token caching
3D Graphics Pipeline WebGL, Three.js, and Babylon.js rendering optimized low-polygon GLB assets
Database & Caching PostgreSQL with row-level tenant isolation and Redis for session cache and rate limits
Security & Tenancy JWT authentication with strict tenant boundary enforcement and role-based permissions

Proven Architecture: SSK Motors & Multi-Brand Dealerships
