Product Overview & Commercial Value

TripeXL.AI is a specialized corporate mobility and fleet governance platform built to streamline the entire lifecycle of corporate ground travel—from employee itinerary submission to administrative cost center allocation.

The platform enforces organizational approval chains before trips are dispatched to fleet drivers. Drivers undergo mandatory pre-trip digital inspections (odometer photo capture, fuel level, vehicle condition checklist) ensuring 100% audit compliance and billing transparency.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Employee Corporate Requestor App

Book point-to-point, airport transfer, or full-day corporate charters with project code tagging, cost center selection, and manager approval tracking.

2. Driver Partner & Inspection App

Turn-by-turn navigation, mandatory pre-trip/post-trip odometer snapshot upload with OCR verification, and electronic passenger pickup sign-off.

3. Multi-Level Management Approval Flow

Hierarchical email and push notification approval workflows routing requests through department heads and travel desk coordinators.

4. Real-Time Telematics & Toll Audit

Continuous GPS dead-reckoning tracking, speed alerts, route deviation warnings, and automated electronic road toll reconciliation.
