Product Overview & Commercial Value

Me2Kart is an enterprise-grade quick-commerce and multi-vendor retail software platform designed to deliver lightning-fast product discovery, instant basket checkout, and localized store fulfillment.

The architecture automatically pins user GPS coordinates to the nearest active dark store or partner supermarket, ensuring that catalog inventory, pricing, and 15-minute delivery promises are 100% accurate before an order is placed.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Instant Basket & Smart Search

Fuzzy search with auto-suggest, barcode scanning, quantity steppers, and single-screen checkout with saved addresses.

2. Geofenced Dark Store Routing

Dynamic customer location bounding box filtering out-of-stock items and routing deliveries to the closest fulfillment hub.

3. Store Picker & Packer App

Tablet and mobile app for dark store pickers with barcode scanning verification and order packing status triggers.

4. Integrated Digital Payments & Split Escrow

UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, cash on delivery, and automated commission splitting for third-party merchants.
