Product Overview & Commercial Value

The Real-Time Cricket Telemetry & Match Prediction Engine is a specialized sports technology solution engineered for fantasy leagues, sports media portals, and fan communities requiring instantaneous updates during high-stakes matches.

Engineered with Flutter and reactive stream controllers, the app decodes live telemetry packets to render ball trajectories, run rate worm graphs, player matchup matrices, and predictive win probability indices in real time.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Sub-Second Ball-by-Ball Live Feed

Zero-delay delivery of over-by-over scorecards, commentary snippets, boundary alerts, and dismissal replays.

2. Dynamic Win Probability Predictor

Algorithmic win percentage index continuously recalculated based on required run rate, wickets in hand, and historical pitch par scores.

3. Interactive Wagon Wheels & Pitch Maps

Custom Canvas vector drawings displaying shot placement angles, bowling pitch lengths, and boundary frequency clusters.

4. Head-to-Head Player Analytics

Detailed batsman vs bowler historical head-to-head match-up statistics, strike rates, and dismissal frequencies.
