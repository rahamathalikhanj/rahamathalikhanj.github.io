On-Demand Multi-App Food & Quick-Commerce Delivery Ecosystem

A high-performance 4-tier delivery platform consisting of Customer Ordering Apps (iOS & Android), Restaurant Kitchen Management, Rider GPS Dispatch, and a Master Administrative Command Center.

Platforms Supported iOS, Android, Web & Tablet Kiosk
Primary Tech Stack Flutter 3.x, Riverpod 2.5, Supabase Realtime, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps SDK
Architecture Status Modular & Multi-Vendor Ready
Deployment Timeline Turnkey Deployment in 2-3 Weeks

Product Overview & Commercial Value

The On-Demand Food Delivery Multi-App Ecosystem is an enterprise-grade digital ordering and logistics infrastructure engineered for cloud kitchens, restaurant aggregators, quick-commerce operators, and grocery chains seeking complete sovereignty over customer ordering, kitchen preparation, and driver dispatch.

Built with modern Flutter cross-platform architecture and Supabase real-time WebSocket state synchronization, the platform coordinates instant order placement, kitchen ticket dispatch, geofenced driver routing, and automated payment escrow with sub-second latency.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Customer Ordering App (iOS & Android)

Interactive menu browsing, dietary filter tags, real-time cart state management with Riverpod, instant checkout via card/wallet/cash, and live turn-by-turn rider GPS order tracking on interactive Google Maps.

2. Restaurant Kitchen Management App

Live POS kitchen order display (KDS), automated acoustic order bell notifications, item 86/out-of-stock toggle switches, prep-time adjustment sliders, and daily revenue reconciliation.

3. Driver Delivery Agent App

Instant push notification dispatch, automated route waypoint calculation, battery-efficient background GPS beaconing, earnings wallet breakdown, and proof-of-delivery photo capture.

4. Master Admin & Control Web Console

Multi-branch store management, commission rate splits, driver onboarding verification queues, heatmaps of active deliveries, promo code engines, and system-wide analytical reporting.

Technical Specifications

Mobile Framework Flutter 3.x (Single unified codebase across Customer, Kitchen, and Driver applications)
State Management Flutter Riverpod 2.5 with asynchronous state notifier providers and immutable models
Real-Time Data Engine Supabase PostgreSQL Realtime channels with row-level security (RLS) policies
Push Notifications Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) with high-priority wake locks and custom sound channels
Geospatial & Mapping Google Maps SDK, Directions API, Places Autocomplete, and geofenced radius queries
Payment Integrations Stripe Connect (automated split payments for platforms & restaurants), Razorpay, and Cash on Delivery

Proven Architecture: Multi-Tier Restaurant & Logistics Operations

This modular four-application architecture eliminates third-party platform commissions (up to 30%), giving food chains and grocery operators direct relationships with their customer base and delivery fleets.
