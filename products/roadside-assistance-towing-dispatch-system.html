On-Demand Roadside Assistance & Tow Truck Dispatch System

A mission-critical emergency mobility software suite enabling stranded motorists to dispatch towing trucks, fuel drop-offs, flat-tire changes, and battery jump-starts with transparent upfront pricing.

Product Overview & Market Need

Vehicle breakdowns on highways and rural roads place motorists in high-stress, dangerous situations. Traditional roadside assistance clubs rely on clunky call centers with long phone holds and uncertain arrival windows.

This solution replaces telephone delays with an instantaneous digital rescue workflow: automatically acquiring the stranded vehicle's coordinates, quoting fixed transparent pricing, and streaming the approaching recovery truck in real-time.

Key Functional Modules

3-Tap Emergency Dispatch

Minimalistic user flow requiring zero typing: auto-detect location, select service type (Towing, Fuel, Jump-Start, Tire), and confirm.

Live Approach Telematics

Continuous GPS tracking of the dispatched service technician, displaying dynamic ETA and route alignment on Google Maps.

Pre-Authorized Escrow Billing

Stripe card pre-authorization places a hold at request time, capturing payment only upon verified roadside service completion.

Crisis Battery Saver Mode

Low-frequency background location polling designed to conserve device power when stranded drivers have critical low battery.

Technical Specifications

Mobile Client Native Android (Java/Kotlin) and cross-platform Flutter support
Location Engine Google Play Services FusedLocationProviderClient with adaptive power profiling
Payment Flow Stripe Payments SDK with pre-authorization holds & digital invoicing
Real-Time Push Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) high-priority alerts with distinctive crisis sound channels
Offline Resilience Local SQLite queue to preserve requests across intermittent cell tower switching
