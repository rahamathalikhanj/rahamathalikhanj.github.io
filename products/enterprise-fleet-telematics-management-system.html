Product Overview & Industrial Value

Corporate logistics teams, mining fleets, and heavy transport operators frequently operate in remote industrial zones, loading basements, and desert highways where mobile signals vanish. Standard cloud-dependent apps fail completely, losing trip logs and disrupting cargo compliance.

FTS Pro implements a true **offline-first local database architecture**. Drivers can conduct vehicle inspections, log cargo handovers, record telemetry, and capture digital supervisor sign-offs 100% disconnected from the internet. When connectivity is restored, the queue syncs with the central server without data collisions.

Key Product Modules

100% Offline Operations

Instant reads and writes powered by the ultra-fast Isar embedded NoSQL database, ensuring uninterrupted fieldwork in dead zones.

Multi-Tier Approval Matrices

Supervisors and safety officers sign off on vehicle release digitally via on-screen signature pads and compliance checklist enforcement.

Fuel & Telemetry Auditing

Automated odometer logging, fuel consumption tracking, engine duty hours, and preventive maintenance notification triggers.

Conflict-Free Auto-Sync

FIFO background queue serialization with timestamped deterministic conflict resolution and payload compression.
