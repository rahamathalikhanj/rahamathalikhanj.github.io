Product Overview & Commercial Value

The Telemedicine & Clinic Patient Management System is an all-in-one healthcare mobility solution designed for medical clinics, individual practitioners, and telemedicine startups to streamline patient triage, appointment booking, and medical history access.

Engineered with a clean, reassuring edge-to-edge Material 3 interface, the system eliminates paper charts through secure electronic health records (EHR), automated SMS/WhatsApp appointment reminders, and digital prescription issuance.

Key Architectural Modules

1. Smart Patient Appointment Booking

Flexible calendar slots, doctor specialty filters, clinic location selectors, and instant confirmation with calendar sync.

2. Electronic Health Records (EHR) & History

Secure cloud repository for lab results, vital sign trend charts, allergic history, and past consultation clinical notes.

3. Digital Prescription & Medication Reminders

Standardized electronic prescription generator with dosage schedules, pharmacy forwarding, and automated patient medication reminders.

4. Doctor Clinical Dashboard

Queue management console showing scheduled appointments, walk-ins, patient medical history, and clinical documentation notes.
